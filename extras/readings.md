# Great Readings

This curated collection gathers milestone treatises, transformative monographs, and field-defining essays in **Metaphysics**. 

Rather than introductory survey textbooks, these works represent the seminal conceptual breakthroughs, systematic frameworks, and dialectical debates that define historical, analytic, and contemporary metaphysical inquiry.

- [Ontology, Categories, and Fundamentality](#ontology-categories-and-fundamentality)
- [Modality, Essence, and Possible Worlds](#modality-essence-and-possible-worlds)
- [Time, Persistence, and Material Constitution](#time-persistence-and-material-constitution)
- [Causation, Laws, and Metaphysics of Science](#causation-laws-and-metaphysics-of-science)
- [Mind, Personal Identity, and Free Will](#mind-personal-identity-and-free-will)

Great Readings

This curated collection gathers milestone treatises, transformative monographs, and field-defining essays in Metaphysics.

Rather than introductory survey textbooks, these works represent the seminal conceptual breakthroughs, systematic frameworks, and dialectical debates that define historical, analytic, and contemporary metaphysical inquiry.

  • Historical Foundations of Metaphysics
  • Ontology, Categories, and Fundamentality
  • Modality, Essence, and Possible Worlds
  • Time, Persistence, and Material Constitution
  • Causation, Laws, and Metaphysics of Science
  • Mind, Personal Identity, and Free Will
  • Social Ontology and Conceptual Engineering
